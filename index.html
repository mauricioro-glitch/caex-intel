About CAEX Intelligence

CAEX Intelligence provides independent analysis of the voluntary carbon market using public registry data. The platform focuses on Verra project activity, issuance trends, retirement behavior, buyer participation and methodology performance.

Our objective is to transform raw registry information into practical market intelligence for project developers, investors, buyers, consultants and policymakers.
