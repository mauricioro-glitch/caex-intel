About CAEX Intelligence

CAEX Intelligence is an independent research project analysing the voluntary carbon market through public registry data. It covers Verra project activity, issuance trends, retirement behaviour, buyer participation and methodology performance.

The aim is to turn raw registry files into something usable by the people who work in this market every day: project developers, investors, buyers, consultants and policymakers.

It is published by CAEX Consultoria, a carbon and environmental consulting firm based in São Paulo, Brazil.
